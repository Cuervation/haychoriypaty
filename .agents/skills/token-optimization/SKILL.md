---
name: token-optimization
description: Size context, delegation, reasoning and validation for a Hay Chori y Paty task; keep routine edits direct and concise.
---

- **SIMPLE** — one localized value/style/text: principal only, minimal local check; no agents, long plan or global suite.
- **MEDIA** — responsive component, route, interaction: implementation stays focused; add an independent validator only when a concrete risk makes it worthwhile.
- **COMPLEJA** — architecture, persistence, economy or cross-system behavior: define acceptance criteria first and require independent `validation`; use `architecture` for bounded design and assign exclusive implementation/validation ownership. No other agents are automatic. Preserve three roles, configured models/efforts and the limit of two concurrent subagents plus the principal.
- **Self-verifying loop when delegated validation is justified:** (1) coordinator records measurable criteria and marks mandatory ones; (2) implementation makes the smallest change and reports paths/check outputs; (3) validation independently checks the original criteria using direct code/test/Unity/capture evidence; (4) coordinator reviews criterion-level status and evidence; (5) for a demonstrated `FAIL`, assign only the named defect to implementation, then have validation rerun affected checks. Reuse unaffected passing evidence.
- Use only `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT_RUN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` (with reason). An implementer's assertion is not independent evidence. Never claim fully validated while a mandatory criterion is `FAIL`, `BLOCKED` or `NOT_RUN`.
- Allow at most **two correction cycles**. If a required criterion still fails or cannot be checked, stop and report the exact evidence, attempts and remaining limitation; do not loop indefinitely or relabel an unrun check as passed.
- Search once for relevant paths/symbols; read the index and owning contract, not entire historical logs. Reuse evidence; avoid duplicate scans, unchanged test reruns and long reports. Give each delegate minimal context, exclusive ownership and notice that others are working; never overlap edits.
- Retain configured role models/efforts. Use the existing low-effort validation and medium implementation/architecture settings; do not silently change the primary chat model/effort. Finish with concise changes, executed checks and risks.
