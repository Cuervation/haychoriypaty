# Gameplay rules

## Current loop

Set an unlocked product's price before starting. Customers automatically enter and group at the standing counter; each requests one product and 1–999 units. Workers reserve individual units, move to a product station, pick up, carry and hand off; each actual handoff decrements one unit and earns its chosen price. Multiple workers and products operate simultaneously without manual movement, flipping or condiment selection. Earned coins buy immediate speed upgrades and extra automatic workers.

Each customer has independent patience, refreshed by deliveries; expiry triggers departure and reservation cancellation. Floresta is a full180-second turn: keep serving/earning after the target and evaluate win/loss at the deadline (24 units by default). Later locations still win early on their delivered-unit target or lose at the clock. Never require completion of every mass order. Progress unlocks products/locations and saves economy/progression. There is no seating or football-result simulation.

Defaults, exact state contracts, persistence and acceptance are authoritative in [features/street-automation.md](features/street-automation.md). The former chori/Coca, shared crowd-patience prototype rules and test record remain historical in [features/floresta-prototype.md](features/floresta-prototype.md), not requirements for the new runtime.
