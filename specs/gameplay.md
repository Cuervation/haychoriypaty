# Gameplay rules

## Current loop

Floresta chori costs$5, fixed: start without price controls. In later levels set an unlocked product's price before starting. Customers automatically enter and group at the standing counter; Floresta supporters request1–4choris, leave after receiving their full request, existing supporters behind advance in the same FIFO column, and incoming supporters join its tail. Later customers request one product and1–999units. Workers reserve individual units, move to a product station, pick up, carry and hand off; each actual handoff decrements one unit and earns its chosen price. Multiple workers and products operate simultaneously without manual movement, flipping or condiment selection. Earned coins buy immediate speed upgrades and extra automatic workers: in Floresta each +10%-of-base speed upgrade costs$25 and each helper$200; later levels retain current balance.

Each customer has independent patience, refreshed by deliveries; expiry triggers departure and reservation cancellation. Floresta has a180-second limit and wins immediately on the1000thactual choripán handoff; below1000at the deadline it loses. The upper-left gold game-coin icon shows current balance; upper-right progress shows sold/1000, for example100/1000. Later locations still win early on their delivered-unit target or lose at the clock. Never require completion of every mass order. Progress unlocks products/locations and saves economy/progression. There is no seating or football-result simulation.

Defaults, exact state contracts, persistence and acceptance are authoritative in [features/street-automation.md](features/street-automation.md). The former chori/Coca, shared crowd-patience prototype rules and test record remain historical in [features/floresta-prototype.md](features/floresta-prototype.md), not requirements for the new runtime.
