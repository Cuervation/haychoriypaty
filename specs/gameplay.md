# Gameplay rules

## Current loop

The **11 traditional modes** (stable IDs 0–8, 10, 12) use the per-product quotas and time limits in [the balance table](../docs/BALANCE_ACTUAL_NIVELES.md); victory requires every unlocked quota, not a total. Exceptions: Camioneros ID9 Tower Defense has no per-product goals or level deadline and ends by fan completion/escape; Huracán ID11 is a 120-second score Bonus Track with no quota. Fixed ID-based prices are Chori0 $5, Paty1 $5, Bondiola2 $10, Vacío3 $12, Coca4 $5, Fernet5 $12, Beer6 $7. A successful real handoff increments only that product and awards its exact price; saved legacy prices and sale prices cannot alter demand cadence or product selection. No price controls are exposed.

Customers have no patience timer/bar or timeout departure; they remain queued until their order completes or the existing sold-out-extras rule releases the lane. In traditional modes, orders retain 1–4 units per product and mix up to five unlocked types; quota deficit/time weights order selection while preserving randomness and variety. Every product enabled by the level catalog may be requested regardless of whether its specialist is hired. Such requests remain pending while the customer waits and can be served after the responsible employee is hired; without that worker, no delivery or revenue is counted. A completed quota remains visible and may generate genuine extra sales. Existing FIFO queues, product stations, physical stock/cooking and specialty assignment remain intact; a player need not clear every customer order. The traditional HUD shows active product progress with icons, delivered/goal and completion state. Tower Defense instead shows coins only, while the Bonus Track uses its score UI. Attempt opening coins and applicable hiring/speed prices follow the balance table, with no carryover from saves or prior attempts. There is no seating or football-result simulation.

Defaults, exact state contracts, persistence and acceptance are authoritative in [features/street-automation.md](features/street-automation.md). The former chori/Coca, shared crowd-patience prototype rules and test record remain historical in [features/floresta-prototype.md](features/floresta-prototype.md), not requirements for the new runtime.


## Five worker roles (current)

Four production specialties/stations derive from the product catalog; the fifth role Mozo is available in the active-cook simulation when an eligible sandwich is in the catalog. The runtime predicate is stable ID `LevelIndex >= 5`: it covers the six later traditional clubs and Huracán ID11. Huracán uses its own bonus-card roster and modifiers; do not infer its free roster from the shared hire curve. Camioneros ID9 is routed through external Tower Defense and does not use the traditional Mozo/active-kitchen loop. Normal cooks handle Chori/Paty; Premium Bondiola/Vacío on a separate grill; Cocacolero Coca/Cerveza; Fernetero exclusively Fernet. Mixed lines run in parallel with specialty-local ownership/FIFO; customers leave only after all lines complete. The first normal Parrillero is free in standard modes; each role then has an independent per-level base cost and paid-tier curve. Traditional hiring, speed and attempt opening balances are defined in the authoritative street-automation contract; each special-mode contract takes precedence for its mode.


## Active kitchen (traditional IDs5+; Huracán has mode-specific bonuses)

[Active cooks and Mozos](features/active-cooks-mozos.md) documents the six traditional club IDs5–8,10,12. The same simulation predicate also enables active cooking for Huracán ID11, with its own roster, cards, multipliers and stock exemption as specified in [its contract](features/huracan-bonus-track.md). Camioneros ID9 does not run this circuit: its external Tower Defense mode has its own worker placement and delivery rules. In the applicable active-cook loop, physical per-worker cooking takes configured activeCookSeconds (2 s at ×1, scaled by CookWorkRate); production and table placement never earn revenue. Sandwiches follow grill → table → Mozo → customer; Cocacolero/Fernetero still deliver their drinks. Hires, speed and each fresh-attempt baseline remain mode-specific; Mercado stock/balance and campaign progression persist as defined by the owning contract.

ARMÁ TU PLANTEL, permanent worker/upgrade unlocks and persistent 5% earnings are not implemented contracts. Do not add them without a new authorized task; they are distinct from existing level progression and Mercado settlement.
